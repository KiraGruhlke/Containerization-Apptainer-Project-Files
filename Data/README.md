## fMRI Dataset

Data provided by Stefan Hetzer. Subject had to tap fingers (left side). 
On the right side the [pulse oxymetry data](pulse_data.csv) was collected.

Each tapping cycle lasts 20 seconds, 10 seconds on, 10 seconds off.

* [1_Localizer_1.5mm_15s.nii](1_Localizer_1.5mm_15s.nii) 
* [2_Anatomy_1mm_5min.nii](2_Anatomy_1mm_5min.nii) hight-resolution anatomy images
* [3_fMRI_TR2sec_3mm_3min.nii](3_fMRI_TR2sec_3mm_3min.nii) low-resolution time series with period 2s
* [4_fMRI_blobs.nii](4_fMRI_blobs.nii) analysed data
